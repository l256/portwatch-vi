        Methodology

        How the progress bar works

        One scale for every project, in two parts. Getting in is a ladder to 50%: groundwork fills the first 10%, then each step needs the one before it, up to Playable. Playing well fills the rest: how far you can play, how right it looks, how fast it runs and how long it keeps running. 100% is the whole game, finished.

          Groundwork, weighted

            Partial steps, such as a menu that renders but is broken, count half. The four tracks only count once a build is Playable: you can't judge how well a game plays before you can play it.
